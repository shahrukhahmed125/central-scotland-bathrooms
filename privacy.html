Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Central Scotland Bathroom Consultancy Ltd (“we”, “us”). We fit wet wall and tiled bathrooms across Central Scotland. We are responsible for any personal information you give us through this website or when you contact us.

You can reach us by phone on +44 7549 348636 or by email at info@centralscotlandbathrooms.co.uk.

What information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us when you get in touch. This can include:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • your town, postcode or address
  • details about your bathroom and the work you’d like done

The enquiry form on our contact page doesn’t send or store anything itself. It opens your own email app or WhatsApp with your details filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.

How we use your information

  • to reply to your enquiry and give you a quote
  • to arrange visits and carry out the work you ask for
  • to keep you updated while your bathroom is fitted
  • to keep the records we need for accounts and tax

We use your information because you’ve asked us to quote for or carry out work (taking steps towards and performing a contract), and because we have a legitimate interest in running our business and keeping proper records. We never sell your information.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to for your job, for example with a supplier delivering goods to your home, or when the law requires it.

If you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp (owned by Meta) also processes your messages under its own privacy policy. Email passes through your email provider and ours.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry. If we carry out work for you, we keep job and payment records for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax purposes, then delete them securely.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or any other tracking.

Our fonts load from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address. Google explains how it handles this in its privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct information that’s wrong or incomplete
  • delete your information, or stop or limit how we use it
  • move your information to another organisation

To make a request, call or email us using the details above. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
